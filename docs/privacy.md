about your data, write to [taverna.claudio@gmail.com](mailto:taverna.claudio@gmail.com).

## Analytics, only if you accept

I use **Google Analytics 4** to see how many people read the blog and which articles they read.
It runs **only if you accept** in the banner: until then the analytics script is not even
downloaded and no cookie is set. If you reject, or close the banner, nothing changes.

If you accept, Google Analytics sets its `_ga` cookies and collects the pages you visit, how you
reached them, your approximate location (country, city), and your device and browser type.
Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. Data is kept for the retention period
set in Google Analytics (14 months) and is processed by Google, also in the United States, under
the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. See [how Google uses this data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage, for about 6 months, after which the banner
asks again. You can change it at any time:

## Other third parties

- **Hosting.** The site is served by [GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement),
  which may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.
- **Fonts, styles and scripts.** To display the pages, your browser downloads fonts from Google
  Fonts and libraries from public CDNs (Bootstrap, Font Awesome, jQuery, jsDelivr). These
  providers receive your IP address as part of the request; they set no cookies.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its processing,
by writing to the address above. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection
authority, the [Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it).
